About

I spend my working life split between two very different terrains. On one, I'm walking clients along ridgelines and glacial valleys as a guide for View Nepal Treks and Expedition — reading weather, altitude, and pace so the mountain stays a good story and not a bad one. On the other, I run a barbershop and handle the marketing engine behind both businesses: shooting and editing the reels, writing the scripts, and keeping the socials moving.

Somewhere in between, I'm also finishing my BCA at Tribhuvan University, which means this site — like most things I build — gets done between treks, haircuts, and assignments. It's not a polished full-time studio. It's one person doing a few things well.
